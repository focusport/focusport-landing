For students and emergencies

Definitely. Students are one of our main user groups. Block social media during study sessions, set automatic focus times during lectures, and track your study streaks. The emergency whitelist keeps Phone and campus apps accessible.
The Emergency App whitelist is designed for this. Before any session, add apps you might genuinely need — Phone, WhatsApp, Maps, banking apps. Those stay fully accessible. Everything else is blocked until time ends.

Permissions and privacy

FocusPort needs Accessibility Service (to detect app switching and block it), Device Admin (to prevent uninstalling during sessions), and Exact Alarm (for scheduled sessions). These are necessary for real blocking — apps that ask for fewer permissions can be easily bypassed.
Android requires Accessibility Service for apps that need to detect and block other apps. FocusPort uses it only during focus sessions to prevent opening distracting apps. Without Accessibility permission, FocusPort cannot block apps.
No. FocusPort does not access or store messages, passwords, photos, contacts, or personal files. The permission is used only for focus enforcement.

Device compatibility

HyperOS may close background services automatically. Autostart allows FocusPort to continue running during an active focus session.
Android manufacturers modify Android with their own battery management systems. Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, and others handle background apps differently. Some devices require additional settings for reliable blocking.
