2. Permissions & Privacy

FocusPort uses Accessibility Service (to detect and block attempts to open distracting apps), Device Admin (to protect against app removal during active sessions), and Exact Alarm (for automated scheduled sessions).
No. FocusPort does not access or store your messages, passwords, photos, contacts, or browsing history. Permissions are used solely for focus enforcement and session tracking.
On Android, FocusPort uses Device Admin and Accessibility permissions to prevent normal attempts to uninstall or remove the application while a focus session is actively running.

3. Device Brands & OEM Optimization

HyperOS and MIUI aggressive battery savers may close background services. Enabling Autostart ensures FocusPort continues running reliably during an active focus session.
Android manufacturers (Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, OnePlus) implement custom background management rules. FocusPort guides you through the necessary manufacturer settings for uninterrupted enforcement.
The Essential App Allowlist lets you choose specific tools (such as Phone, SMS, work messaging, or study utilities) that remain accessible during your focus session while all distracting apps remain strictly locked.
