No magic. Just Android permissions used responsibly.

Uses Android Accessibility Service — to detect when you try to switch apps during a session. This is how it blocks Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and any app you choose.

Blocks selected apps during sessions — not your whole phone. Calls, messages, and apps you whitelist stay available. Everything else waits until the timer ends.
