What you can't do during a session

Once a timer starts, FocusPort holds on. This is what happens when you try to quit.

Strict app blocker features

Every feature is designed to protect your focus and support your routine.

Allowlist lock

Select only the specific apps you need for work while blocking access to the rest of your device.

Browser protection

Automatically blocks YouTube Shorts, adult sites, and common distracting keywords if a web browser is allowed.

Scheduled and remote sessions

Set sessions to start automatically at specific times or trigger them remotely.

Cross-device sessions (beta)

Starting a session on your phone can simultaneously lock your Windows PC.

Goals, streaks and reports

Define your task goal before starting and review your focus history and streaks over time.

Add time mid-session

Extend your focus period in 15-minute increments without interrupting your workflow.

Ambient sounds

Play background sounds such as rain, fire, ocean, or forest to aid concentration.

One session across your phone and PC

Synchronize your focus environment across your devices during active sessions. See how FocusPort works on Windows.
